01 / The why

Awareness is where

There Is Only Room for Heart is a breast cancer awareness campaign built around a simple belief: information matters, conversations matter, and people deserve to feel supported.

Through community events, educational resources, and opportunities to get involved, the campaign aims to make space for more informed, compassionate conversations about breast cancer.

THE HEART BEHIND THE CAMPAIGN

A reason close
to my heart.

Nihal Alakbarova · Founder

Some causes become personal before we fully understand how deeply they will shape us. For me, breast cancer awareness became one of them when I lost a close family member to the disease.

Their loss changed the way I thought about breast cancer and the importance of awareness. It made me want to do something meaningful—not because I could change what had happened, but because I could help create opportunities for others to learn, recognize warning signs, and seek medical advice sooner.

That is why I started There Is Only Room for Heart. I want this campaign to encourage conversations that might otherwise go unspoken, make reliable information more accessible, and remind people that paying attention to their health matters. Through awareness initiatives, community events, and education, I hope to help more people understand the importance of early detection and seeking medical advice when something feels wrong.

This campaign is deeply personal to me, but its purpose reaches beyond my own experience. It is about turning loss into action, compassion into community, and awareness into something that can make a difference.

There is only room for heart because every life matters, every conversation matters, and every opportunity to seek care matters.
